# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2, 2026

## Overview
This application is open-source software that runs entirely on your local device. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. Because we believe your data belongs to you, **this application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry of any kind.**

---

## Data Processing & Collection

### 1. Local Processing
All data processed by this application remains strictly on your local hardware. No information processed by the software is ever transmitted to us or any third-party servers.

### 2. No Analytics or Telemetry
The software contains zero tracking scripts, analytics tools, error-reporting services, or background telemetry. We do not track:
* Who uses the application
* How or when the application is used
* System metrics, IP addresses, or hardware details
* Crash reports or diagnostic logs

### 3. Third-Party API Interactions
If you configure the application to interact directly with third-party services (such as YouTube or Google APIs), those interactions occur directly between your local device and the respective third-party endpoints using your own credentials or API keys. 
* We do not operate an intermediary server.
* We do not proxy, intercept, or log any traffic or authorization tokens generated during these interactions.
* Third-party API usage is governed solely by the privacy policies of those respective services (e.g., [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

---

## Source Code & Verification
Because this project is open source, you can inspect the source code, build the application yourself, or audit network traffic at any time to verify that no data is being collected or sent.

---

## Changes to This Policy
Because our software architecture does not collect data or rely on centralized servers, our privacy principles will not change. If we update this document, the updated version will be committed directly to our open-source repository with a revised "Last Updated" date.

If you have any questions or security concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please open an issue in the official project repository.
